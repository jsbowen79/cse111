1. What is the title of your program? 

Checkbook Register with Balancing Tool

2. What real-world problem will your program address or help to solve?
Programs like Quicken and Quickbooks charge a monthly fee to 
keep registers and balance them.  Many people can't afford to pay
a monthly subscription in order to be able to balance their checkbook. 
This program will allow a user to keep a check register and then help 
to automatically balance the register with their statements.  

3. What will you learn from developing this program?
I will make an attempt to scan a pdf of a bank or credit card statement, 
extract only the needed information and use it to automatically balance 
the register. This will require me to learn a lot about parsing pdf files
for the needed information.  I will also have to become very proficient 
with libraries and file management.
